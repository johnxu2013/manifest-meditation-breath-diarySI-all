🛡️ Your Privacy is Our Priority

Manifest all in one is designed with privacy at its core. All your health data is processed locally on your device and remains under your complete control. We do not collect, store, or share your personal data with third parties.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Sleep Tracking Data

When you use Manifest all in one's sleep tracking features, the app collects the following data locally on your device:

  • Motion Data: Accelerometer readings to detect movement patterns during sleep
  • Ambient Sound: Sound levels (measured in decibels) to assess sleep environment
  • Sleep Metrics: Derived data including sleep stages, duration, efficiency, and quality scores
  • Timestamps: Start and end times of sleep sessions

1.2 Journal & Wellness Data

  • Journal Entries: Text you write in the personal journal feature
  • Mood Information: Mood tracking data you choose to record
  • Daily Checklists: Tasks and completion status you create
  • Calendar Events: Wellness-related events and reminders you schedule

1.3 App Usage Data

  • Feature Usage: Which app features you use (stored locally only)
  • Settings Preferences: Your app configuration and preferences
  • Meditation & Breathing Sessions: Duration and frequency of practice

2. How We Use Your Information

All data processing happens entirely on your device, while you may choose to store sleep in icloud with health. We use your information to:

Purpose How It Works
Sleep Analysis Process sensor data to estimate sleep stages and calculate quality metrics
Smart Alarms Monitor your sleep state to find optimal wake-up times
Trend Analysis Generate charts and insights based on your historical data
Health Integration Sync sleep data with Apple Health (only with your explicit permission)
Cloud Sync Store your data in your private iCloud account (only if enabled)

3. Data Storage and Security

3.1 Local Storage

Your data is stored securely on your device using:

  • SwiftData: Apple's modern data persistence framework with encryption
  • Secure Enclave: Hardware-level security on supported devices
  • App Sandbox: Isolated storage that only Sleep Health Tracker can access

3.2 iCloud Sync (Optional)

If you enable iCloud sync:

  • Your data is stored in your private CloudKit container
  • Data is encrypted in transit and at rest
  • Only you can access your data through your Apple ID
  • We cannot access your iCloud data
  • You can disable sync at any time in Settings

3.3 Apple Health Integration (Optional)

When you enable Apple Health integration:

  • Sleep data is synced to your Health app
  • Governed by Apple's strict HealthKit privacy policies
  • You control exactly what data is shared
  • Can be revoked at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Health

4. Data Sharing and Third Parties

✅ What We Do NOT Do

  • We do NOT sell your personal data
  • We do NOT share your data with advertisers
  • We do NOT send your data to external servers
  • We do NOT use your data for marketing purposes
  • We do NOT track you across other apps or websites
  • We do NOT collect personally identifiable information

Third-Party Services: Manifest all in one does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. Your data stays between you and Apple's secure ecosystem.

5. Your Privacy Rights

You have complete control over your data:

5.1 Access Your Data

  • View all your sleep sessions in the History tab
  • Review trends and analytics at any time
  • Access journal entries and checklists

5.2 Export Your Data

  • Export sleep data to Apple Health
  • Data is available through Health app's export feature
  • Use standard iOS sharing to backup journal entries

5.3 Delete Your Data

  • Delete individual sleep sessions from the History view
  • Clear all data using "Clear All Local Data" in Settings
  • Uninstalling the app removes all local data
  • Delete iCloud data separately in Settings → iCloud

5.4 Control Data Sharing

  • Enable/disable iCloud sync in Settings
  • Control Health app permissions in iOS Settings
  • Revoke microphone and motion permissions in iOS Settings

6. Permissions Required

Manifest all in one requests the following permissions to function properly:

Permission Purpose Required
Motion & Fitness Access accelerometer for movement detection during sleep Yes (for sleep tracking)
Microphone Monitor ambient sound levels (no recording is saved) Yes (for sleep tracking)
Notifications Send smart alarm and reminder notifications Yes (for alarms)
Apple Health Read and write sleep data to Health app Optional
iCloud Sync data across your Apple devices Optional

Important: We only request permissions necessary for app functionality. You can revoke permissions at any time through iOS Settings, though this may limit certain features.

7. Audio Recording

🎤 Microphone Usage Clarification

Sleep Health Tracker does NOT record or save audio.

The microphone is used only to measure ambient sound levels (volume in decibels). No audio recordings are created, stored, or transmitted. Only numerical sound level measurements are saved to help assess your sleep environment.

8. Diary

Diary data are installed locally, and it will be viewable from private calendar, which kept private calendar events in device only. If user sets up local SI servers in this app, the rewriting data can be kept in local server.

9. Data Retention

  • Local Data: Stored indefinitely until you delete it or uninstall the app
  • iCloud Data: Stored in your iCloud account as long as you have the app installed and sync enabled
  • Health App Data: Stored according to Apple Health's retention policies (controlled by you in the Health app)
  • No Server Storage: We do not store any of your data on our servers

10. International Users

Sleep Health Tracker is designed to comply with privacy laws worldwide, including:

  • GDPR (European Union): Your data processing stays on your device and Apple's infrastructure
  • CCPA (California): You have full control to access, export, and delete your data
  • PIPEDA (Canada): Consent-based data handling with full transparency

Since all processing happens locally on your device and in your private iCloud account, your data remains in your geographic region according to Apple's data residency policies.
